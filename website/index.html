Privacy notice

Before any student records

CLEAR keeps a first name, a feeling, the choice a student tapped, the time, and the teacher’s yes or no. It does not ask for an address, phone number, photo, or student ID. The week stays on the teacher device. This page shows it only while it is open, and it is not sent to a company server.

These notes are school records. FERPA expects the school to keep them for the classroom and to let a parent look at a child’s records and ask for a correction or deletion. COPPA’s school rule fits this page: it is used for the class, not as a public site for children, and a parent can refuse by asking the teacher not to keep that child’s panel or this browser’s copy.

  • The teacher device asks for a class PIN, then a teacher code, before it shows messages or shares its week over USB.
  • CLEAR.CSV stays on the teacher device, locked with the class PIN. This page reads it over USB only after the connected device accepts the class PIN and teacher code.
  • After five wrong tries, the teacher device waits a minute before it takes codes again.
  • A student panel must use that class PIN. The teacher then allows that panel. Other panels stay on a waiting list, and their messages are not saved.
  • Names and messages on this page hide after 5 minutes with no mouse or keyboard activity. The graphs that do not name a student stay up.
  • Student panel settings go from this browser straight to that panel over USB or Bluetooth. They are not sent to a server. The settings password is not saved here.
  • This page does not save codes or weeks. Locking or closing the page clears them. Deleting CLEAR.CSV on the teacher device clears that device’s week.
